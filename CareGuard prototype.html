How it works

No new device for your parents. Just a phone call from a friendly voice.

1

We call, they chat

A 3 to 5 minute call at their preferred time, in Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam or English. It asks about meals, medicines, sleep and mood, and keeps them company.

2

You get a clear update

One WhatsApp message each day, plus a dashboard that shows trends in mood, sleep and medicine habits over the week and month.

3

We act if something is wrong

Missed calls and worrying answers trigger a step-by-step response. Premium adds emergency coordination.

  3. 15 minutesYou get a calm message on WhatsApp: "We are checking on Amma."
  4. 20 minutesApartment security or a neighbour you nominated does a visit and reports back.
